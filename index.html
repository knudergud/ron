About

Sixth of seven, first of his name

I grew up at The Burrow in Ottery St Catchpole, Devon — the sixth of seven Weasley children, which teaches you two things fast: how to make an entrance and how to hold your own. I went to Hogwarts (1991–1997), where I was sorted into Gryffindor, kept goal for the Quidditch team, served as prefect, and co-founded Dumbledore’s Army.

After the war I trained as an Auror and served in the Ministry’s Auror Department alongside Harry Potter, helping rebuild the department from the ground up. These days I co-manage Weasleys’ Wizard Wheezes in Diagon Alley with my brother George, where I handle strategy, logistics, and the occasional demonstration-gone-wrong.

What I bring to any team: calm under fire, a talent for reading a board three moves before anyone else sees the threat, and a proven record of showing up — for mates, for family, and for the odd flying car.

Experience

Where I’ve made a difference

  1. 2000 – present

    Co-manager · Weasleys’ Wizard Wheezes, Diagon Alley

    Run the shop floor, product pipeline, and wholesale logistics with my brother George. Grew the Skiving Snackbox line into an international brand and kept the fireworks licence (mostly) intact through three Ministry inspections.

  2. 1998 – 2000

    Auror · Department of Magical Law Enforcement, Ministry of Magic

    Joined the rebuilt Auror Office with Harry Potter straight out of the war. Tracked down the last of the dark wizards, testified at dozens of trials, and helped reform recruitment and training for the next generation of Aurors.

  3. 1995 – 1998

    Founding member · Dumbledore’s Army

    Co-founded the DA and served as second-in-command through its defence-against-the-dark-arts resistance years, including the Battle of Hogwarts, where I fought alongside the defenders and helped evacuate the wounded under fire.

  4. 1991 – 1997

    Student, Prefect & Keeper · Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry

    Five O.W.L.s, Gryffindor prefect, and Keeper for the house Quidditch team — capped by the 1996 Quidditch Cup, where I held three saves against Ravenclaw in the final. Also undefeated at wizard chess in the common room for six straight years.

Selected work

Projects I’m proud of

1997 – 1998 · Field operation

The Horcrux Hunt

A year-long, off-books operation to locate and destroy the Dark Lord’s Horcruxes. Ran point on planning, camp logistics and morale; personally destroyed the locket and the cup. Outcome: mission complete, all seven targets neutralised, everyone came home.

Outcome: dark wizard defeated, wizarding world restored. Slightly understaffed the whole time.

1996 · Quidditch

Gryffindor Quidditch Cup

Starting Keeper in the 1995–96 season after a rough trial run. Held my nerve in the final against Ravenclaw with three saves in the first ten minutes and played through a team badly out of sorts to win the Cup.

Outcome: Gryffindor won the Cup. My brother’s commentary remains legendary.

2000 – present · Retail & product

Weasleys’ Wizard Wheezes expansion

Took the shop from a beloved local joke shop to a Diagon Alley institution with an international owl-order business. Own the logistics, staffing, and the Peruvian Instant Darkness Powder supply chain.

Outcome: year-over-year growth, zero catastrophic explosions since 2003.

1992 · Transport (unauthorised)

The Ford Anglia Flight

Located and operated a flying Ford Anglia to an unreachable destination under a tight deadline and heavy fog. Flawless landing (on the first tree). Vehicle later went feral in the Forbidden Forest, which I maintain was not my fault.

Outcome: arrived on time. Received a Howler. Worth it.
